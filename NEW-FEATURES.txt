STAFF POINTS (new)
  /staff lb        weekly staff points leaderboard (resets every Sunday 12pm)
  /staff all-time  same leaderboard, never resets
  Dropdown under the board: Staff Points / Ticket Closes / Ticket Claims / Ticket Renames / Schematic Posts

LOA + CLOSE REQUESTS (new)
  /loa request days:<1-30> reason:<text>   goes to the LOA channel with Accept / Deny; Accept gives the LOA role until time is up
  Set the LOA role id in extraConfig.js -> loa.roleId (channel + max days are there too)
  Ticket close: staff pressing Close (or /close, /ticket close) asks the ticket owner "solved / helped?" with Close / Deny.
    Owner closing their own ticket just closes it. Deny = ask again after 20s. The staff member who ASKED gets the close point.
  Schematic posting: 20s cooldown, DM after ~10 spammed posts.
  Files: commands/loa.js, utils/loaManager.js, events/loa.js, events/closeRequest.js, commands/ticket.js, utils/ticketManager.js, extraConfig.js
